How this works

A 3D scan of the robotics room. Walk around, find your team's things, and tag what happens to them before the move.

  1. Move: click the orange arrows on the floor (or press the arrow keys) to hop between 9 spots. Click a dot on the map to jump. Drag to look around.
  2. Tag: press Tag an area, then either drag a Box, or pick Shape and click points around the thing (click the first point or press Enter to finish; drag to draw freehand). The map works out the 3D object inside and highlights it. Pick your team (Skyrunners, RoboSub, Escape Room, General, Previous teams) and a status: moving out by a date, no longer needed, or long-term storage.
  3. Save: you're sent to GitHub with everything filled in. Press Submit new issue. Your pin shows as dashed until it's saved, then everyone sees it.
  4. Change or remove a tag: click its pin → Edit on GitHub. Tagged areas stay outlined in the room as you move around.
